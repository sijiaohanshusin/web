# Fictional Member Wall Photos

These four images were generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for the isolated
member-wall design demo. They depict fictional people and workspaces, not actual
association members. They are outside the static/media directories and served
only by DEBUG-only routes. Do not import them into the production roster or the
member asset library.

Prompt set: portrait 3:5 editorial documentary photography, warm electronics
workbench lighting, graphite/copper/teal palette, natural anatomy and safe tool
handling, upper-two-thirds composition and darker lower desk. No text, UI,
badges, borders, branding or watermarks.

- `soldering.png`: close-up hand soldering a green circuit board.
- `maker.png`: fictional adult student in a charcoal hoodie, side/back profile.
- `signal.png`: handmade signal generator and oscilloscope with a sine wave.
- `builder.png`: fictional adult student in glasses assembling a small board.
